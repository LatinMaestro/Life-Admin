## Full Description

Family Life Admin gives your household one simple place to keep everyday admin organised.

Use it together to:

- see what needs attention today;
- create and assign household tasks and reminders;
- share shopping lists;
- keep bills and renewals visible as due-date reminders;
- collect inbox items and route them to the right place;
- link back to original source documents and websites;
- add comments and review household activity;
- manage household members and roles.

The app is designed for families and shared households, with clear screens and only the actions you need. Information is kept within your household workspace.

Bills and renewals are reminders, not financial tracking. Family Life Admin does not provide budgets, bank connections, transaction tracking, cashflow analysis, tax reporting, or payment details. Payment-related reminders point back to the original source selected by the household.

A Family Life Admin account and internet connection are required.

## Review Notes

Family Life Admin is a private, login-required household administration app. The reviewer account will be supplied privately in the review console and must contain fictional data only.

Suggested review path:

1. Sign in with the supplied reviewer account.
2. Open the Rivera Household.
3. Review Home, Tasks, Shopping, Bills, Family, and Settings.
4. Create and complete a task.
5. Add and check a shopping item.
6. Open a bill reminder and follow its fictional source link.
7. Review account deletion in Settings.

Bills are administrative reminders only. The app intentionally does not display or store payment identifiers. Camera, microphone, file upload, OCR, AI, email forwarding, and push notifications are not part of this release.

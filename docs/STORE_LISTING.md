## Full Description

Family Life Admin gives your household one simple place to keep everyday admin organised.

Use it together to:

- see what needs attention today;
- create and assign household tasks and reminders;
- share shopping lists;
- keep bills and renewals visible as due-date reminders;
- collect inbox items and route them to the right place;
- attach or capture private household sources and return to the original;
- forward selected household email for review-first intake;
- turn selected documents or voice notes into drafts for a household member to review;
- choose opt-in reminders for supported household items;
- add comments and review household activity;
- manage household members and roles.

Automated intake is bounded and review-first. It suggests a draft; an authorised household member decides whether to save or change it. Manual entry remains available.

Bills and renewals are administrative reminders, not financial tracking. Family Life Admin does not provide budgets, bank connections, transaction tracking, cashflow analysis, tax reporting, payment execution, or payment identifiers. Reminders return the household to its original source.

A Family Life Admin account and internet connection are required. Camera, photo-library, microphone, and notification access is optional and requested only when a user chooses the related feature.

## Review Notes

Family Life Admin is a private, login-required household administration app. Supply the reviewer account only in App Store Connect and use fictional data.

Suggested review path:

1. Sign in with the supplied reviewer account.
2. Open the Rivera Household.
3. Review Home, Tasks, Shopping, Bills, Family, Inbox, Sources, and Settings.
4. Create and complete a task, then add and check a shopping item.
5. Open a bill reminder and follow its fictional original-source reference.
6. Choose a fictional image/document source and inspect the reviewable draft.
7. Start a short fictional voice draft, review it, and discard or confirm it.
8. Inspect notification preferences; permission is requested only after explicit opt-in.
9. Review privacy controls and account deletion in Settings.

Optional intake features require a deliberate user action. Camera/photo selection, uploaded sources, forwarded email, OCR/classification, and voice processing create bounded reviewable drafts only. Manual entry is always available. The app intentionally rejects and does not store, transcribe, reproduce, log, or notify structured payment identifiers.
